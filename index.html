How to Play:

  • Click a piece to select it (it will be highlighted)
  • Click a valid square to move
  • Diagonal moves only on dark squares
  • Jump over opponent pieces to capture them
  • Reach the opposite end to become a King (can move backward)
  • First player to capture all opponent pieces wins!
